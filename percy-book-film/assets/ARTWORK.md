# Lesson artwork and content notes

The seven original WebP images were supplied in Percy_Jackson_Book_and_Film_Quest.zip. They are lesson illustrations, not verified frames from the 2010 film.

storm-escape.jpg is an original generated illustration for Chapter 4, My Mother Teaches Me Bullfighting: Sally drives the red Camaro through a storm with Percy and Grover while the Minotaur follows. The source PNG is preserved in the author workspace under .codex/generated_images.

Book scenes use the museum/fountain (Chapter 1), the Fates (Chapter 2), Grover at the cabin (Chapter 3), storm escape and Minotaur (Chapter 4). The Olympus illustration belongs only to the 2010-film comparison. The finale returns to Chapter 4 artwork.

Content review references:
- Rick Riordan teacher guide: https://rickriordan.com/resource/the-lightning-thief-a-teachers-guide/
- Novel text, Chapter 4: https://studylib.net/doc/27026027/lightning-thief-the--percy-jac---rick-riordan

Scope: chapters 1-4 and the opening scenes of the 2010 film. Exact film timestamps vary by release and opening logos. No scenes from the television adaptation are used as film evidence.
