📖 Modal Verbs Cheat Sheet (open any time)

can / can't — ability now: I can repair it.
could / couldn't — ability in the past, or possibility: He couldn't fix it yesterday.
be able to — ability, often in other tenses: We will be able to test it tomorrow.
must / have to — obligation: Workers must wear helmets.
mustn't — forbidden: You mustn't touch the wires.
don't have to — not necessary: You don't have to stay late.
should / shouldn't — advice: You should check the engine.
might / could — possible: The pipe might be blocked.

Remember: mustn't ≠ don't have to.
