🎓 Teacher guide — learn Brawl Stars in 2 minutes

Brawler = playable fighter. Main attack = standard attack. Super = powerful special move. Gadget = extra ability. Star Power = passive ability. Hypercharge = temporary boost. Gem Grab = collect and hold gems; Brawl Ball = score goals; Showdown = survive; Heist = attack the enemy safe and defend yours.

Timing: teach me 6 min; character detective 6; robot building 11; drawing 8; arena design 8; battle pitch 6. Ask “Why?”, “How would that work?”, “Is it fair?”, “What if…?”. Let the pupil teach you the mechanics.
