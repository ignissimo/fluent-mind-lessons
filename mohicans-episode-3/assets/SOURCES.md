## Story

Episode description checked 2 November 2026: https://www.thetvdb.com/series/the-last-of-the-mohicans-1/episodes/4635038

David is an entomologist in this animated adaptation. The vocabulary, timeline and past-tense tasks follow the supplied episode description. The role-play and diary are invented learning activities inspired by the episode; they are not transcripts. The full video has not been reviewed. The waterfall is discussed as an event in fiction, not as a real-life instruction.

The approximate 30-minute lesson follows viewing the episode; viewing time is additional. This is an estimate, not a timed learner trial. Notebook writing and oral responses are reviewed by the teacher; checkboxes are confirmations, not automatic assessment of the writing.
