# Campfire Secrets — Chapter 7

An English lesson inspired by Rick Riordan’s *The Lightning Thief*, Chapter 7, “My Dinner Goes Up in Smoke”. The story order and chapter boundaries were checked against the [Chapter 7 summary at LitCharts](https://www.litcharts.com/lit/the-lightning-thief/chapter-7) and [Rick Riordan’s teacher’s guide](https://rickriordan.com/resource/the-lightning-thief-a-teachers-guide/). The lesson does not reveal the godly parent or events from later chapters. Practice sentences are chapter-inspired examples, not a verbatim extract.

## Illustrations

Original book-inspired interpretations generated with OpenAI ImageGen for Fluent Mind Adventures on 23 November 2026. They are illustrations of the novel’s scenes and do not depict screen actors.

- offering.jpg — Percy places a portion of his food into a bronze brazier at the open-air dining pavilion.
- lake.jpg — Percy and Annabeth see freshwater naiads beneath the lake’s surface. Naiads have human legs and wear ordinary clothes.

Original PNGs are retained outside the published repository in the Codex generated_images folder. Published JPEGs are resized proportionally without cropping.
