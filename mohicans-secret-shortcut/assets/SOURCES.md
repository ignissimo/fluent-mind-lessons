# The Secret Shortcut — artwork and story sources

Unofficial educational activity based on Mondo TV / Rai Fiction's animated series *The Last of the Mohicans* (L'ultimo dei Mohicani), not the live-action films or the original novel. Original series artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. Images are not AI-generated.

- `travellers.jpg`: Hawkeye, Big Snake and Uncas; TheTVDB episode 1 thumbnail. https://artworks.thetvdb.com/banners/v4/episode/4635036/screencap/637f8ec0e233b.jpg
- `cora.jpg`: Cora; TheTVDB episode 2 thumbnail. https://artworks.thetvdb.com/banners/v4/episode/4635037/screencap/637f8ef3c00ad.jpg
- Episode index and plot checked 26 October 2026: https://www.thetvdb.com/series/the-last-of-the-mohicans-1/allseasons/official
- Cross-check: https://www.imdb.com/title/tt15062854/episodes/?season=1

The frames illustrate the series and characters; they are not presented as exact frames of each exercise. Episode 1 is used only for the cover; chapter 1 of the learning game has not been reviewed or changed. Missions 5 and 6 are invented activities inspired by episode 2. Classroom timing excludes watching the 25-minute episode.
