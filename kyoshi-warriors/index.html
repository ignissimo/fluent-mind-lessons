Rules, Choices & Consequences · Modal Verbs Quest

A2–B1 · 40–45 minutes · 6 missions · Write first, speak when ready

Abilities: can / can't / could / be able to
Obligations: must / have to
Forbidden: mustn't
Not necessary: don't have to
Advice: should / shouldn't / ought to
Deduction: must = almost certain, might/could = possible, can't = impossible.

Remember: mustn't ≠ don't have to!
