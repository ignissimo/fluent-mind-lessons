Mission 2 — Three Verbs, Two Meanings

Some verbs can describe a state or an action. The meaning helps us choose the tense.

1. THINK

Present Simple — opinion

I think Toph is right.

This is my opinion.
Present Continuous — mental activity

I am thinking about Toph's decision.

I am spending time thinking about it now.

2. HAVE

Present Simple — possession

Sokka has a boomerang.

He owns it.
Present Continuous — activity

They are having dinner.

They are eating now.

3. SEE

Present Simple — vision

I see Azula in the distance.

I can see her with my eyes.
Present Continuous — meeting

We are seeing our friends tonight.

We have arranged a meeting.

Choose the correct form

These sentences are practice examples, not quotes from the episode.

Fix the mistakes: Write the correct sentences.

Mission 3 — A Difficult Night

Read the story. You can return to the text when answering.

Aang and his friends are travelling through the Earth Kingdom. They are very tired because Azula and her team keep following them. Whenever they stop to rest, their enemies find them again.

Katara thinks everyone should help with the camp. Toph wants to be independent and believes she can take care of herself. They argue because they do not agree. The group is tired, so it is difficult for them to listen calmly.

Later, Toph meets Iroh. He listens to her and explains that accepting help does not mean being weak. Toph starts thinking about his advice. At the same time, Aang tries to find a way to stop the chase so his friends can finally rest.
