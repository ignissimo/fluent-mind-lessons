3 · Build Your World 8 min

Choose a tile, then tap the map. Place one hero 🧑 and one exit 🚩. Add at least one star ⭐ if your rule requires collecting all stars. Add walls, enemies and bushes.

🎤 Explain your map: Where is the most dangerous place? Can the player win without fighting?

4 · Invent the Rules 7 min

The attack idea and extra rule are for your design pitch. They do not change this prototype.

🎤 Why are these rules fair? Is your game too easy or too difficult?

5 · Playtest & Improve 12 min

Use arrow keys or buttons. Your teacher is the first game tester.

6 · Game Pitch 5 min

Present your game to your first investor — your teacher!

🎤 Pitch: “My game is called... The player must... Unlike other games... I improved... because...”

Teacher Guide · Click to open

Suggested pacing: Idea 5, Hero 8, World 8, Rules 7, Playtest 12, Pitch 5 minutes. Let the student explain decisions aloud before advancing.

Speaking: “Why did you choose that?”, “What happens if…?”, “How would a beginner win?”, “Is this fair?”, “Can you improve it?”

Gameplay: The hero moves one tile at a time. Walls and water block movement; bushes are passable. Enemy tiles cause damage or block movement depending on the rule. Stars are collected; the flag is the exit. This is a playable prototype, not a full commercial game engine.

English targets: because, if, unless, unlike, should, could, fair, challenge, collect, avoid, improve, ability, weakness.
