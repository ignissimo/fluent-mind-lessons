Mythology Archive

Identify the gods, spirits and ancient tradition.

Burnt offerings

At dinner, the campers give the best part of their food to the gods. In ancient Greek religion, people burned part of a sacrifice as an offering. Percy cannot name his father, so his offering becomes a silent request: Tell me who you are.

Match the names and descriptions

Think: Why must the campers give the gods the best food, not the food they dislike?

The Campfire Challenge

Welcome a new demigod and explain the camp.
Your role: You are an experienced camper. Your partner plays a new demigod who is confused and worried.

The new camper asks:

  1. Where am I?
  2. Why am I here?
  3. Can monsters enter the camp?
  4. Can I go home?
  5. What happens at dinner?
