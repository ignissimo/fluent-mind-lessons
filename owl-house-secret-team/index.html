Mission 4 · Vocabulary Match

Mission 5 · Grammar: Although / Because / So

Because gives a reason. So gives a result. Although introduces a surprising contrast.

Hunter joined the team because he wanted to recruit students.
He started to care about his teammates, so his plans changed.
Although he had a secret mission, he made real friends.

Optional speaking: Say one true sentence about your life using although.

Mission 6 · Your Character's Secret

Write 5–6 connected sentences. Say your ideas aloud first. Then write them as one short paragraph.

I think...because...although...For example,...In the end,...

You can use the sentence starters, but you don't have to. Your ideas matter more than perfect grammar.

Final Challenge · Predict the Future

Choose one question and make a prediction. Use only what you know after this episode. Different predictions are welcome.

Speak: “I think ... might ... because ...”

Optional: draw a scene from the next episode on paper and explain it.
